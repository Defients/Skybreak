# Skyward Ascent — The Card Game Rulebook

*A tabletop dungeon crawl you play with a standard deck of cards · An Astrizda Game*

---

# Welcome

Skyward Ascent is a solitaire dungeon crawler designed to be played with nothing more than a standard 52-card deck, two six-sided dice, and a handful of tokens. You lead a party of three Heroes through a three-tier ascent of **the Astrilith**, fighting monsters, collecting loot, and ultimately facing **Vyridian, the Astril Conductor** at the summit.

The digital version is a 1:1 simulation of these tabletop rules. Everything you can do in the app, you can do at your kitchen table.

---

# 1. What You Need

## 1.1 Required Materials

- **One standard 52-card deck** (plus 2 Jokers)
- **2 six-sided dice (2d6)** — for action rolls and random decisions
- **4 twenty-sided dice (4d20)** or pen & paper — for tracking Hero and monster HP
- **Colored tokens** (6 types, 6 of each):
  - 🔵 Blue — Shields
  - 🔴 Red — Target markers
  - ⚫ Black — Buffs
  - 🟡 Yellow — Debuffs
  - 🟢 Green — Counters
  - ⚪ White — Special markers
- **Gold tracker** — pen & paper, spare coins, or a d20
- **Direction marker** — a coin or token to indicate turn direction
- **Position markers** — numbered 1 through 4

## 1.2 Deck Preparation

Sort your deck into four piles:

| Pile | Cards | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Royalty Deck** | Jacks, Queens, Kings, Aces of all suits | 16 |
| **Peon Deck** | Cards 2–10 of all suits | 36 |
| **Joker Cards** | Both Jokers | 2 |
| **Environment Deck** | 4 cards (one of each suit) from unused Hero Royalty | 4 |

Shuffle each pile separately. Keep them face-down within reach.

## 1.3 Card Hierarchy

When comparing card values:

> **A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2**

# 2. Setting Up Your Party

## 2.1 Determining Classes

Each Hero's class is determined by drawing cards:

1. **Separate** the Peon Deck by rank: pull all 3s, all 5s, all 7s, and all 9s into four small piles.
2. **Shuffle** each pile independently.
3. **Draw one card** from each pile. You now have four cards, each with a rank and suit.
4. **Rank determines class**, **suit determines specialization**:

| Rank | Class | Base HP | Black Spec (♣️/♠️) | Red Spec (♦️/♥️) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | Bladedancer | 16 | Shadowblade | Runeblade |
| 5 | Manipulator | 17 | Timebender | Illusionist |
| 7 | Tracker | 16 | Huntmaster | Beastcaller |
| 9 | Guardian | 18 | Sentinel | Warden |

5. **Choose 3** of the 4 drawn classes for your party. The 4th unused class's suit cards become your **Environment Deck** source.
6. **Return** the drawn cards to the Peon Deck and reshuffle.

## 2.2 Party Formation

1. Arrange your 3 Heroes in a row from left to right. Assign them **positions 1, 2, and 3**.
2. Each Hero has a **Hero ID** matching their position number.
3. Place each Hero's class card face-up in front of you, with their position marker on it.
4. Set each Hero's HP tracker (d20 or written) to their class base HP.

## 2.3 Starting Resources

Each Hero receives:

| Class | Starting Gold | Starting Item |
|---|---|---|
| Bladedancer | 40g | Minor Potion |
| Manipulator | 40g | Mystic Rune |
| Tracker | 40g | Lucky Charm |
| Guardian | 40g | Shield Charm |

**Total party gold:** 120g (for 3 Heroes). Gold is shared — keep it in one pool.

Each Hero begins with a **Common weapon** for their class (your choice of which Common weapon).

## 2.4 Welcome Bonus

Each Hero rolls 2d6 and consults the table:

| 2d6 Roll | Reward |
|---|---|
| 2–5 | 20g |
| 6–8 | Common weapon for their class (your choice) |
| 9–10 | Common weapon (your choice) + 20g |
| 11–12 | Rare weapon for their class (your choice) |

Place any won weapons under the corresponding Hero's class card. Add gold to the party pool.

# 4. Room Types

## 4.1 Merchant Room

- No combat occurs.
- Browse the shop: buy items, weapons, enchantments, upgrades, healing services, and weapon services.
- Prices scale by current tier (see Section 9).
- You may leave when ready.
- If your inventory is over-capacity, you must resolve it before leaving (sell or discard items).

## 4.2 Combat Room

- Fight one standard monster (draw from Royalty Deck).
- Gain gold and potential loot on victory.

## 4.3 Elite Combat Room

- Fight one monster with enhanced stats:
  - +5 HP
  - +1 APC
  - Better loot table
- Enhanced gold rewards.

## 4.4 Rest Room

Choose **one** of the following:

1. **Fully heal** all Heroes.
2. **Revive** one dead Hero to 50% HP, and fully heal all other Heroes.
3. **Gain gold**: roll 2d6 × 10 × current tier.
4. All Heroes **gain +2 max HP** (permanent).

## 4.5 Mini-Boss Room

- Fight one unique mini-boss (see Section 11).
- Guaranteed Rare+ weapon drop on victory.
- Bonus gold: 100g × current tier.

## 4.6 Final Boss Room

- Face **Vyridian, the Astril Conductor** (see Section 12).
- Victory wins the run if at least one Hero survives.

# 5. Combat Setup

When you enter a combat room, follow these steps:

## Step 1 — Reveal the Monster

1. Draw the top card from the **Royalty Deck**.
2. The card determines the monster (see Section 10):
   - **Jack** = Common monster
   - **Queen** = Uncommon monster
   - **King** = Rare monster
   - **Ace** = Elite monster
3. The suit determines which specific monster of that tier.
4. Calculate monster HP: **Base HP + 3 × tier number**. Set the monster's HP tracker.
5. Note the monster's special ability (triggered on Match — see Section 6).

## Step 2 — Assign APCs

**APC** (Attached Peon Card) is a Peon card placed under a Hero or Monster card. It represents that entity's action capability.

1. **Shuffle** the Peon Deck.
2. Each Hero **draws 1 APC** — place a Peon card face-down under their class card.
3. The monster **draws 2 APCs** — place 2 Peon cards face-down under the monster card.
4. **Elite monsters** draw +1 APC (3 total).
5. **Mini-Bosses** draw 3 APCs.
6. **Vyridian** draws 4 APCs.
7. If any entity has 4+ APCs of the same rank, reshuffle and redraw that entity's APCs.

> **Tip:** Place APCs slightly fanned under the card so you can see their ranks without flipping them yet. APCs are **face-down** — you know how many you have but not their ranks until a Match occurs.

## Step 3 — Environment Effect

1. Flip the top card of the **Environment Deck**.
2. The suit determines the environment for this entire combat:

| Suit | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| ♣️ | Training Ground | No effect |
| ♦️ | Library | All entities gain +1 APC (draw an extra Peon card each) |
| ❤️ | Armory | Heroes gain +2 to first roll; Monster gains +1 to first 3 rolls |
| ♠️ | Elemental Chamber | Black specs gain +1 to all rolls; Red specs gain +1 APC |

3. Place the Environment card face-up near the battlefield. It stays active for the entire combat.

## Step 4 — Choose Hero Turn Order

1. Choose the order in which your Heroes will act each round.
2. This order is **locked** for the entire combat — you cannot change it mid-fight.
3. Place your Heroes in their chosen order (rearrange position markers if helpful).

# 6. The Match System

Matches are the heart of combat. They trigger special abilities and bonuses.

## 6.1 How Matches Work

During each turn, the active entity **flips 2 Peon cards** from the Peon Deck. After both are flipped, check for matches:

- A **Match** occurs when a flipped card's **rank** matches the rank of one of that entity's **APCs**.
- Check both flipped cards against all APCs.
- A matching APC can trigger **once per flip pair**.
- The same APC cannot trigger twice from the same pair.
- Multiple different APCs may trigger from one flip pair.

## 6.2 Match Types

| Match Type | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Match** | One flipped card matches one APC | Trigger that entity's matched ability |
| **Double Match** | Both flipped cards match different APCs | Trigger both abilities; +1 damage to next attack |
| **Set Match** | Both flipped cards match the same APC rank | +1 to next roll |
| **Chain Match** | 3+ consecutive match events across turns | +2 to next roll |

## 6.3 What Matches Do

- **For Heroes:** A Match triggers your Hero's class ability (marked ⚛️). This can happen **once per combat** unless otherwise specified.
- **For Monsters:** A Match triggers the monster's special ability.
- After resolving matches, **discard** the flipped Peon cards to a discard pile. APCs remain in place.

# 7. Combat Flow

Combat proceeds in **rounds**. Each round:

1. **Monster turn** (see 7.1)
2. **Hero turns** in chosen order (see 7.2)
3. **Summoned creatures** act after their controller
4. **End of round** — check victory/defeat

Repeat until the monster is dead (victory) or all Heroes are dead (defeat).

## 7.1 Monster Turn

1. **Start-of-turn effects:** Apply any tokens that trigger at turn start (Poison damage, Burn damage, Regeneration healing, etc.).
2. **Check if prevented:** If the monster has a debuff that prevents action (Petrify, Stun, Freeze with failed roll), skip its action and go to cleanup.
3. **Flip 2 Peon cards** from the Peon Deck.
4. **Check matches** against the monster's APCs.
5. **Resolve special ability** if a match occurs.
6. **Roll 1d6** for the monster's action. Consult the monster's roll table (Section 10).
7. **Apply roll modifiers** (Fear: -1, Haste: +1, etc.).
8. **Resolve the action** — deal damage, apply tokens, heal, summon, etc.
9. **End-of-turn cleanup** — decrement token durations, remove expired effects.

## 7.2 Hero Turn

1. **Start-of-turn effects:** Apply Poison damage, Burn damage, Regeneration healing, etc.
2. **Check if prevented:** If the Hero has Petrify, Stun, or Freeze (must roll 4+ on a d6 to act), skip action and go to cleanup.
3. **Pre-flip choices:** You may declare item use or pre-flip abilities now (if allowed by the item/ability).
4. **Flip 2 Peon cards** from the Peon Deck.
5. **Check matches** against the Hero's APCs.
6. **Resolve Hero ability** if a class-card match occurs and the ability is available (once per combat).
7. **Resolve weapon/enchantment effects** if applicable.
8. **Roll 1d6** for the Hero's action. Consult their class roll table (Section 8).
9. **Apply roll modifiers** (weapon bonuses, enchantments, tokens, environment, match bonuses).
10. **Resolve the action** — deal damage, heal, gain shields, etc.
11. **End-of-turn cleanup** — decrement token durations, remove expired effects.

## 7.3 Damage Calculation

Calculate damage in this order:

```
Base Damage (from roll table)
+ Weapon Bonuses
+ Enchantment Bonuses
+ Token Modifiers (Target: +1, Might: +2, etc.)
+ Environment Modifiers
+ Match Bonuses (Double Match: +1, Chain: +2)
- Shield Reduction (🔵 tokens)
- Armor/Defense Abilities
= Final Damage (minimum 0)
```

> **Phase-through damage** ignores ALL damage reduction — shields, armor, and defense abilities do not apply.

## 7.4 Combat Cleanup

After combat ends:

1. **Remove** all temporary effects and tokens (except 🟢 Counters).
2. **Return** temporary APCs to the Peon Deck.
3. **Collect rewards** (gold, items, weapons).
4. **Shuffle** the Peon Deck and Royalty Deck discard back into their decks.
5. Continue to the next room.

# 9. Death and Revival

## 9.1 When a Hero Dies

A Hero at **0 HP** is dead. When a Hero dies:

- Remove them from active positioning (they can't be targeted).
- Their **position is reserved** — if revived, they return to the same spot.
- They **skip all turns**.
- They **lose all tokens** except 🟢 Counters.
- They **cannot vote** on split path decisions.
- They are **not counted** as a party member.

## 9.2 Revival

A dead Hero can be revived by:

- **Rest Room** choice (revive to 50% HP)
- **Healing Services** at the Merchant (Revive 50% or Revive Full)
- **Guardian Angel** item (auto-revive at 50% HP when lethal damage would occur)

When revived, the Hero returns to their position, restores HP as specified, and may act on future turns.

> **Healing cannot revive dead Heroes** unless the effect specifically says "revive."

# 13. Items and Merchant

## 13.1 Inventory Rules

- Each Hero has **3 item slots** plus their equipped weapon.
- Joker cards count as **Major Potions** and use inventory slots.
- **Permanent upgrades** do not count against the item limit.
- The party has **2 shared inventory slots** (any Hero can use these items).
- Over-capacity must be resolved before leaving the Merchant.
- Items sell for **25%** of purchase price.
- Weapons and permanent upgrades cannot be discarded.

## 13.2 Consumable Items

| Item | T1/T2/T3 Cost | Effect | Stack Limit |
|---|---|---|---|

- Monster HP = **Base HP + 3 × tier number**

# 19. Quick Reference

## 19.1 Combat Turn Summary

**Monster Turn:**
1. Start-of-turn effects → 2. Flip 2 Peon cards → 3. Check matches → 4. Roll d6 → 5. Apply modifiers → 6. Resolve action → 7. End-of-turn cleanup

**Hero Turn:**
1. Start-of-turn effects → 2. Pre-flip items/abilities → 3. Flip 2 Peon cards → 4. Check matches → 5. Resolve ability/weapon → 6. Roll d6 → 7. Apply modifiers → 8. Resolve action → 9. End-of-turn cleanup

## 19.2 Damage Formula

```
Base + Weapon + Enchant + Tokens + Environment + Matches - Shields - Armor = Final Damage
```

Minimum final damage is 0. Phase-through damage ignores all reduction.

## 19.3 Target Priority (when not specified)

1. Lowest or highest HP (as specified by the ability).
2. Most or fewest tokens (as specified).
3. Lowest Hero ID (for Heroes) or highest Monster ID (for monsters).
4. Roll d6 — highest wins.

## 19.4 Token Duration Quick Reference

| Token | Duration |
|---|---|
| 🔵 Shield | Until absorbed |
| 🔴 Target | 3 turns |
| ⚫ Buff | As specified (usually 1–3 uses/turns) |
| 🟡 Debuff | As specified (usually 1–3 turns) |
| 🟢 Counter | Permanent until spent |
| ⚪ Special | Unique per ability |

## 19.5 Key Reminders

- **Gold is shared** by the party and cannot go negative.
- **Healing cannot exceed max HP** and cannot revive dead Heroes.
- **Round down** all calculations unless stated otherwise.
- **Match abilities trigger once per combat** (Heroes) unless otherwise specified.
- **Temporary APCs** are removed at end of combat.
- **Dead Heroes** keep their position for revival but lose all tokens except 🟢 Counters.
- **No turn limit** in combat, but environmental hazards begin after 10 rounds if needed.
- **Stalemate** means Heroes retreat and receive no rewards.
